ESP32-C6 WiFi Repeater

Install the firmware straight from your browser. Nothing to download or install.

This browser cannot flash over USB. Use Chrome, Edge or Opera on a computer, or flash with esptool. The installer needs a secure (https) page.
  1. Plug the ESP32-C6 into your computer with a USB data cable.
  2. Click Install, pick the serial port of the board, and confirm.
  3. When it finishes, connect to the WiFi ESP32-Repeater (password changeme123) and open http://192.168.4.1/ (user admin).
  4. Enter your router's name and password, then change the passwords.

Ticking Erase device in the installer also wipes your saved settings. Leave it unticked to keep them when updating. On Linux, your user must be allowed to use the serial port (sudo usermod -aG dialout $USER, then log in again).

Source code, documentation and releases: github.com/jmleclercq/esp32-wifi-repeater
